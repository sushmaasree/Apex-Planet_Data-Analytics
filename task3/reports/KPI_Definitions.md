## KPI #1: DAILY REVENUE RUN RATE (DRR)

### Definition
The average daily revenue generated by the coffee shop, used to forecast monthly and annual performance.

### Formula
```
Daily Revenue Run Rate = Total Revenue / Number of Operating Days

DRR = Σ(Transaction Amount) / Count(Unique Dates)
```

### Calculation from Dataset
```
Total Revenue: $112,245.58
Operating Days: 381 days
DRR = $112,245.58 / 381 = $294.61 per day
```

### Business Rationale
- **Why it matters:** Predicts future revenue and identifies performance trends
- **Decision support:** 
  - Budget planning and forecasting
  - Identifying seasonal patterns
  - Setting realistic growth targets
  - Staffing and inventory decisions

### Benchmarks & Targets
- **Current Performance:** $294.61/day
- **Target (Growth 15%):** $339/day
- **Warning Threshold:** <$250/day (15% below average)
- **Critical Threshold:** <$200/day (requires immediate action)

### Trends Observed
- **Peak Days:** October avg $448/day, March avg $294/day
- **Low Days:** April avg $191/day, January avg $229/day
- **Seasonality:** Q1 and Q4 strongest, Q2 weakest

### Actionable Insights
- 📈 **Opportunity:** Increase Q2 performance (currently 35% below Q1)
- 🎯 **Action:** Launch spring promotions, seasonal drinks
- 📊 **Monitor:** Daily vs weekly vs monthly trends

## KPI #2: AVERAGE TRANSACTION VALUE (ATV)

### Definition
The average amount spent per customer transaction, indicating pricing effectiveness and upsell success.

### Formula
```
Average Transaction Value = Total Revenue / Total Transactions

ATV = Σ(Transaction Amount) / Count(Transactions)
```

### Calculation from Dataset
```
Total Revenue: $112,245.58
Total Transactions: 3,547
ATV = $112,245.58 / 3,547 = $31.65
```

### Business Rationale
- **Why it matters:** Measures revenue per customer visit and upsell effectiveness
- **Decision support:**
  - Pricing strategy validation
  - Product bundling opportunities
  - Staff training effectiveness
  - Premium product promotion success

### Benchmarks & Targets
- **Current Performance:** $31.65
- **Target (Growth 10%):** $34.82
- **Industry Benchmark:** $28-35 (coffee shops)
- **Premium Segment:** $35+ per transaction

### Segmentation Analysis
- **By Price Category:**
  - Premium transactions: $35.76 avg (39.9% of sales)
  - Mid-range transactions: $32.82 avg (26.2% of sales)
  - Standard transactions: $27.92 avg (33.9% of sales)

- **By Time of Day:**
  - Morning: $30.42 avg
  - Afternoon: $31.64 avg
  - Night: $32.89 avg (+8% vs morning)

### Actionable Insights
- 📈 **Opportunity:** Night shows highest ATV - promote evening visits
- 🎯 **Action:** Bundle deals for morning customers to increase ATV
- 💡 **Strategy:** Train staff on upselling premium products (currently 46% of revenue from 40% of transactions)

## KPI #3: PRODUCT MIX PERFORMANCE (PMP)

### Definition
The distribution of revenue across product categories, measuring portfolio health and diversification.

### Formula
```
Product Mix Performance = (Category Revenue / Total Revenue) × 100

PMP_category = (Σ Revenue_category / Σ Total Revenue) × 100
```

### Calculation from Dataset
```
Coffee - Milk: $76,450.42 / $112,245.58 = 68.1%
Coffee - Black: $17,340.54 / $112,245.58 = 15.4%
Non-Coffee: $18,454.62 / $112,245.58 = 16.4%
```

### Business Rationale
- **Why it matters:** Ensures balanced revenue streams and reduces dependency risk
- **Decision support:**
  - Product development priorities
  - Inventory allocation
  - Marketing focus areas
  - Menu optimization

### Current Mix Analysis
| Category | Revenue | % of Total | Units Sold | Avg Price |
|----------|---------|------------|------------|-----------|
| Coffee - Milk | $76,450 | 68.1% | 2,339 | $32.68 |
| Non-Coffee | $18,455 | 16.4% | 515 | $35.83 |
| Coffee - Black | $17,341 | 15.4% | 693 | $25.02 |

### Benchmarks & Targets
- **Current:** 68% Coffee-Milk, 16% Non-Coffee, 15% Black Coffee
- **Target:** 65% Coffee-Milk, 20% Non-Coffee, 15% Black Coffee
- **Rationale:** Increase higher-margin non-coffee items

### Risk Assessment
- ⚠️ **High dependency:** 68% revenue from one category
- ✅ **Opportunity:** Non-coffee items have highest average price ($35.83)
- 📊 **Trend:** Evening non-coffee sales +138% vs morning

### Actionable Insights
- 📈 **Opportunity:** Grow non-coffee category from 16% to 20%
- 🎯 **Action:** Evening marketing for hot chocolate/cocoa
- 💡 **Strategy:** Introduce seasonal non-coffee items

## KPI #4: PEAK HOUR UTILIZATION RATE (PHUR)

### Definition
The percentage of total daily transactions occurring during identified peak hours, measuring operational efficiency.

### Formula
```
Peak Hour Utilization Rate = (Peak Hour Transactions / Total Daily Transactions) × 100

PHUR = (Σ Transactions_peak_hours / Σ Total Transactions) × 100
```

### Calculation from Dataset
```
Peak Hours Identified: 10 AM (323), 16 PM (298), 14 PM (277)
Total Peak Hour Transactions: 898
Total Transactions: 3,547
PHUR = (898 / 3,547) × 100 = 25.3%
```

### Business Rationale
- **Why it matters:** Optimizes staffing, reduces wait times, maximizes revenue
- **Decision support:**
  - Staff scheduling optimization
  - Capacity planning
  - Queue management
  - Customer satisfaction improvement

### Hourly Distribution Analysis
| Hour Range | Transactions | % of Total | Classification |
|------------|--------------|------------|----------------|
| 6-9 AM | 512 | 14.4% | Early Morning |
| 10-11 AM | 599 | 16.9% | **Peak Morning** |
| 12-13 PM | 449 | 12.7% | Lunch Rush |
| 14-16 PM | 840 | 23.7% | **Peak Afternoon** |
| 17-19 PM | 691 | 19.5% | Evening |
| 20-22 PM | 456 | 12.9% | Late Night |

### Benchmarks & Targets
- **Current PHUR:** 25.3% (top 3 hours)
- **Target:** Maintain 24-28% peak concentration
- **Off-Peak Target:** Increase 6-9 AM and 20-22 PM by 20%

### Operational Insights
**Staffing Recommendations:**
- **Peak Hours (10-11 AM, 14-16 PM):** 3-4 staff members
- **Standard Hours:** 2-3 staff members
- **Off-Peak Hours:** 1-2 staff members

### Actionable Insights
- 📈 **Opportunity:** Off-peak hours (6-9 AM, 20-22 PM) represent 27.3% capacity
- 🎯 **Action:** Happy hour pricing 7-9 AM and 8-10 PM
- 💡 **Strategy:** Loyalty rewards for off-peak visits

## KPI #5: REVENUE CONSISTENCY INDEX (RCI)

### Definition
A measure of revenue stability across different time periods, indicating business predictability and risk.

### Formula
```
Revenue Consistency Index = 1 - (Standard Deviation / Mean)

RCI = 1 - (σ_revenue / μ_revenue)

Where higher RCI (closer to 1) indicates more consistent revenue
```

### Calculation from Dataset
```
Daily Revenue Mean: $294.61
Daily Revenue Std Dev: $89.47
RCI = 1 - (89.47 / 294.61) = 0.696 = 69.6%

Transaction Amount Mean: $31.65
Transaction Amount Std Dev: $4.88
Transaction RCI = 1 - (4.88 / 31.65) = 0.846 = 84.6%
```

### Business Rationale
- **Why it matters:** Predicts cash flow stability and reduces forecasting risk
- **Decision support:**
  - Financial planning
  - Inventory management
  - Risk assessment
  - Investment decisions

### Consistency Analysis

**Daily Revenue Consistency: 69.6%**
- Interpretation: Moderate consistency, some variability
- Factors: Seasonality, day of week effects
- Risk Level: Medium

**Transaction Value Consistency: 84.6%**
- Interpretation: High consistency, stable pricing
- Factors: Fixed product prices, balanced mix
- Risk Level: Low

**By Time Period:**
| Period | RCI | Interpretation |
|--------|-----|----------------|
| Weekday vs Weekend | 92.3% | Very consistent |
| Morning/Afternoon/Night | 95.7% | Highly consistent |
| Month-to-Month | 65.4% | Moderate (seasonal) |

### Benchmarks & Targets
- **Current RCI:** 69.6% (daily revenue)
- **Target:** 75%+ (reduced variability)
- **Industry Benchmark:** 65-80% for retail/food service
- **Best Practice:** 80%+ indicates mature, stable business

### Risk Factors
- ⚠️ **Seasonal Variation:** Q2 -35% below Q1
- ⚠️ **Day of Week:** Sunday -27% below Tuesday
- ✅ **Time of Day:** Highly balanced (95.7% RCI)

### Actionable Insights
- 📈 **Opportunity:** Improve monthly consistency (currently 65.4%)
- 🎯 **Action:** Seasonal promotions to smooth Q2 dip
- 💡 **Strategy:** Weekend-specific bundles to boost Sunday performance
