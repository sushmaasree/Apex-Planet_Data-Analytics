## ANALYSIS HIGHLIGHTS

### Top Findings
1. **Revenue:** $112,245.58 total, $31.65 average transaction
2. **Best Product:** Americano with Milk (22.8% of sales)
3. **Peak Performance:** Tuesday, 10 AM, Afternoon period
4. **Growth Opportunity:** Weekend revenue (25% → 30% target)
5. **Premium Impact:** 40% of transactions = 46% of revenue

### Key Patterns
- Balanced time-of-day distribution (32-34% each period)
- Consistent weekday performance (14-16% each day)
- Strong Q1 performance (31.6% of annual revenue)
- Coffee-Milk category dominates (66% volume, 68% revenue)
- Evening non-coffee items 138% higher than morning

### Business Recommendations
1. Implement off-peak promotions (6-8 AM, 8-10 PM)
2. Launch weekend bundle offers
3. Evening hot chocolate marketing campaign
4. Premium product upselling training
5. Optimize staffing for peak hours
